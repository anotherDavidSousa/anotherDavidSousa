# 🚀 Featured Case Studies

## 🚚 Fleet Management Platform

> A logistics operations platform designed to centralize fleet, driver, trip and tracking information.

[![Django](https://img.shields.io/badge/Django-092E20?style=flat\&logo=django\&logoColor=white)](https://www.djangoproject.com/)
[![PostgreSQL](https://img.shields.io/badge/PostgreSQL-4169E1?style=flat\&logo=postgresql\&logoColor=white)](https://www.postgresql.org/)
[![Docker](https://img.shields.io/badge/Docker-2496ED?style=flat\&logo=docker\&logoColor=white)](https://www.docker.com/)
[![n8n](https://img.shields.io/badge/n8n-EA4B71?style=flat\&logo=n8n\&logoColor=white)](https://n8n.io/)

### The problem

Logistics information was distributed across different systems and operational processes.

Vehicles, drivers, trailers, documents, tracking information and daily operations needed to be correlated to provide a clearer operational view.

### What I built

* Fleet and vehicle management
* Driver and owner management
* Trailer assignment
* Operational timeline
* Vehicle tracking integration
* Geofences and map visualization
* Operational dashboards and KPIs
* CT-e / transportation document integrations
* Driver matching and data correlation
* Automation workflows with n8n
* Containerized infrastructure

### Engineering topics

`Django` · `PostgreSQL` · `Docker` · `REST APIs` · `n8n` · `Leaflet` · `Chart.js` · `Nginx`

➡️ **[Explore the Fleet Management Case Study](https://github.com/anotherDavidSousa/fleet-management-case-study)**

## 🤖 WhatsApp Logistics Automation

> Automation designed to allow drivers to access operational documents without needing to visit the office.

### The problem

Drivers depended on manual office interaction to obtain information required during logistics operations.

### What I built

A workflow capable of:

* Identifying registered drivers
* Retrieving operational information
* Applying business rules
* Generating transportation documents
* Returning information through WhatsApp
* Reducing unnecessary office interaction

The solution combined messaging automation, structured data and business rules to simplify the driver's workflow.

### Engineering topics

`Python` · `Automation` · `WhatsApp` · `APIs` · `Google Sheets` · `AI Integration`

➡️ **[Explore the WhatsApp Automation Case Study](https://github.com/anotherDavidSousa/whatsapp-automation-case-study)**

# 🧭 How I Approach Projects

I like to understand software beyond the implementation itself.

When working on a project, I try to think about:

**Problem**

What real problem are we trying to solve?

↓

**Process**

How does the operation work today?

↓

**Architecture**

How should responsibilities, data and integrations be organized?

↓

**Implementation**

Which technologies make sense for the problem?

↓

**Automation**

What repetitive work can be eliminated?

↓

**Observability**

How can users understand what is happening?

↓

**Iteration**

What did the project teach me and what should improve next?
